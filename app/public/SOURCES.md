# Product photo sources

Catalog names, KRW prices, sizes and bundles are synthetic demo data, not live Coupang listings. Images are downloaded locally. No Coupang scraping. Each resized JPEG retains the source image license. Fun SVGs are original project art.

Open Food Facts / Open Beauty Facts photos: CC BY-SA 3.0; attribution below links to individual product pages and contributor history. Database fields adapted from these projects are ODbL; demo pricing and IDs are original. Source brand labels do not imply endorsement.

Representative photos can be shared by size or bundle variants. Wikimedia photos show the product type; they are not retailer listing photos. The patches item uses a representative repellent photo and is the explicit exception.
