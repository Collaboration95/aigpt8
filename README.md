# Team 8

## Click · 척하면 척 (*cheok-hamyeon cheok*)

**Name: Click.** In English, two people "click" when they understand each other instantly, which is the meaning of 척하면 척. It's also what you do in the app: every answer is a tap, never a form.

**Tagline:** 척하면 척, the shop that gets you before you finish.

**What it means:** this Korean idiom describes someone who understands you from the smallest hint. You barely start ("척"), and they already know ("척"). It's how you describe a friend or partner who just gets it, with no long explanation needed.

**Why it fits:**
- **Context:** "reorder my toothpaste" is enough, because the app already knows your usual. "Mosquitoes" is enough, because it remembers your King bed (✦ from your March order) and asks only what it can't infer.
- **Effortless:** you never fill in forms. You answer with taps, and the screen builds itself for that moment.
- **Learning:** a 30-second game teaches it your taste, so the next "척" needs even less.

**English renderings for slides:** "Gets you before you finish." / "Half a word is enough."
**Pronunciation:** chuk-ha-myun chuk.
