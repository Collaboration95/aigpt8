## Direct slide guidance

The exact challenge is: “Reimagine and create an AI-first retail experience that shifts ecommerce from browsing and searching to AI as the primary interface, fundamentally changing how customers discover, evaluate, and purchase products.” The explicit stages are DISCOVER → EVALUATE → PURCHASE. This sets a stronger direction than adding an AI feature to conventional ecommerce. [S01](text-slides/S01-main-challenge.txt) (IMG01/IMG03)

S03 reinforces: “Not a feature added to ecommerce. A new way to shop, designed around AI.” Its three closing questions are:

- **HUMAN:** “Is it effortless?”
- **INTELLIGENT:** “Does context compound?”
- **TRUSTED:** “Can customers stay in control?”

These are design prompts; no scoring weights or prescribed architecture accompany them. [S03](text-slides/S03-your-turn.txt) (IMG04)

S02 says: “Start with the customer's context, then let AI orchestrate the journey.” It does not specify that context must come from prior usage history. [S02](text-slides/S02-possibility-space.txt) (IMG02)

## EXAMPLES — possibilities, not mandates

S02 explicitly labels itself POSSIBILITY SPACE and “EXAMPLE: WHAT COULD AI-CORE SHOPPING FEEL LIKE?” Its mappings and examples are:

- **VOICE → Strong Intent:** a request combining cheapest price, red color tone and socks matching the customer's “Nike Super[obscured]”. The product model, remaining request and full left journey cannot be recovered.
- **VISION → Weak Intent:** pictures of the customer's home, car or outfit provide context; products are recommended with pictures. The example journey is SEE → MATCH → FIT.
- **Personalize → No Intent - Discovery:** create a premade video or pictures of the user and product. The example journey is EXPLORE → UNLOCK → BUY.

These illustrate possibilities; they do not require those intent/modality mappings, all three features, shoes, any provider or social login. [S02](text-slides/S02-possibility-space.txt)

S03's dense illustration includes DISCOVER, TRY ON, CHECKOUT, IN STORE and BEYOND. Its readable BEYOND caption mentions post-purchase care for a longer journey, extending the illustrated possibilities beyond checkout. Treat these as possibilities, not a scope checklist or reliable guarantees. Purchase is explicit in the main challenge, but the photographs specify no native checkout, payment or autonomous purchase implementation. [S03](text-slides/S03-your-turn.txt); [S01](text-slides/S01-main-challenge.txt)
