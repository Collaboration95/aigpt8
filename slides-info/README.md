Four user-supplied photographs, preserved unchanged in `sources/`. `SOURCE-MANIFEST.json` maps IMG01–IMG04 to original filenames, byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes. IMG01 and IMG03 appear to show the same main-challenge slide; IMG02 shows possibility-space examples; IMG04 shows the closing design prompts. Printed section numbers and page numbers are separate labels.

Start with the [extracted slide information](SLIDES-INFO.md). Faithful text-only slides are [S01: main challenge](text-slides/S01-main-challenge.txt), [S02: possibility-space examples](text-slides/S02-possibility-space.txt), and [S03: closing prompts](text-slides/S03-your-turn.txt).

All photo transcriptions finished before information extraction began. `pass1/` contains per-photo plain text and a separate independent visual reading. `text-slides/` contains verified text for the three distinct slides. `pass2/` records [text reconciliation](pass2/TEXT-RECONCILIATION.md) and the [extraction check](pass2/EXTRACTION-CHECK.md). See [RUN-LOG.md](RUN-LOG.md) for task ownership and results.

Transcription convention: reproduce visible wording, spelling and reading order. Plain-text labels may identify columns or graphic regions, but bracketed notes such as `[obscured]`, `[unreadable]`, and `[uncertain: …]` are editorial, never slide text. Do not complete covered words from familiar product names, infer speaker identities from people in photos, or add the earlier oral transcript to missing pixels. Decorative diagram text is transcribed only where legible. No image enhancement or regenerated slide imagery is part of this task.

The official written brief and explicit organizer clarification govern event rules. Example slides remain examples, and design prompts do not establish scoring weights, submission requirements, or permissions. Earlier transcript evidence is kept separately in `../briefing/`.
